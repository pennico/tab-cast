Tab Cast for Mac: installing it on your Mac
===========================================

Tab Cast for Mac sends a window, the whole screen, or a second screen of your Mac (picture and sound) to a TV that runs
Tab Cast TV. It works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13 or later.

1. Unzip "Tab Cast for Mac ….zip" (double-click it) and drag "Tab Cast" into the Applications folder.

2. Open it the first time:
   - Double-click "Tab Cast" in Applications. macOS says it can't check the app (it isn't from the App Store and
     isn't notarized by Apple). Click Done (or Cancel), do not move it to the Bin.
   - Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Tab Cast was blocked…" and click Open Anyway, then
     Open. macOS asks this only once. (On macOS 13 or 14: right-click "Tab Cast" › Open › Open also works.)
   - A TV icon appears in the menu bar at the top right (there is no Dock icon).

3. Check this Mac (once, about 3 seconds):
   TV icon › Check This Mac for Casting… The result is saved in Downloads as "Tab Cast Mac check ….txt" and
   says the best quality this Mac can send. Send that file back if you were asked for it. It holds no names,
   serial numbers or addresses.

4. Connect to the TV (the Mac must be on the same Wi-Fi as the TV, with Tab Cast TV open on it):
   TV icon › Add the TV… and type the TV's address as Tab Cast TV's start screen shows it. Then type the 6-digit code the TV shows.
   If the TV shows no code, choose "Pair a device" on the TV.

5. Cast:
   - Use the TV as a Second Screen: the TV becomes a screen of its own, right of the Mac's. Put the game on it with
     TV icon › Move a Window to the TV. The Mac's own screen stays free.
   - Cast a Window / Cast the Whole Screen: the TV shows exactly that.
   macOS asks once for Screen & System Audio Recording, and for Accessibility the first time you move a window.
   Allow them, then choose the menu item again.

6. Stop: TV icon › Stop Casting.

The game's sound plays on the TV and the Mac stays silent while it does (macOS 14.2 or later; on older macOS the Mac
plays it too: turn the Mac's volume down).
